# Landing Page Requirements for AI Agent Service

## Target Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Crypto enthusiasts, traders, investors, and DeFi participants seeking advanced market intelligence and trend analysis.
- **Secondary Audience**: Developers, analysts, and institutions in the blockchain and Web3 space looking for actionable insights on projects, protocols, and narratives.
- **User Profiles**:
  - Individual retail traders (18-45 years old) interested in staying ahead of market trends.
  - Professional investors and fund managers needing data-driven decision-making tools.
  - Blockchain developers and researchers exploring emerging technologies like DeFi, NFTs, L1/L2s, and AI integrations.

## Key Messaging
- **Headline**: "Unlock Crypto's Future with AI-Powered Market Intelligence – Identify Narratives Before They Explode"
- **Subheadline**: "Get ahead of the curve with real-time insights, momentum scoring, and trend analysis from our advanced AI agent."
- **Core Theme**: Empowering users with predictive analytics and exclusive knowledge in the fast-paced crypto market.
- **Tone**: Confident, innovative, and accessible – blending technical expertise with user-friendly language to build trust and excitement.

## Value Propositions
- **Early Access to Narratives**: Discover emerging crypto trends and narratives before they become mainstream, giving users a competitive edge.
- **Actionable Insights**: Receive data-driven recommendations on projects, protocols, and market opportunities, reducing risk and maximizing returns.
- **Real-Time Momentum Scoring**: Leverage AI-generated scores to identify high-potential projects and track their social and market traction.
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Access in-depth analysis on DeFi, NFTs, L1/L2s, AI agents, RWAs, and more, all tailored to user needs.
- **Cost-Effective Intelligence**: Efficient, pay-per-insight model that delivers high-value information without overwhelming costs.
- **Integration and Scalability**: Designed for seamless integration with trading tools, portfolios, and DevOps workflows in cloud environments.

## Core Features to Highlight
- **Narrative Analysis**: Detailed breakdowns of trends like restaking, AI agents, RWAs, Bitcoin L2s, and memecoins, including growth potential and risks.
- **Project Momentum Tracking**: Lists of trending projects with scores, filters by name, ticker, or Twitter handle, sorted by momentum or popularity.
- **Market Intelligence Chat**: Interactive AI agent for querying specific crypto topics, providing personalized insights and responses.
- **Trending Projects Dashboard**: Real-time top projects based on momentum scores, with thresholds for high-growth opportunities.
- **Historical and Predictive Data**: Insights backed by ML models analyzing market data, social signals, and protocol metrics.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Simple API access or web interface for quick queries, suitable for both novices and experts.
- **Security and Privacy**: Emphasis on secure, cached data handling to protect user information and ensure reliable, up-to-date intelligence.

## Additional Considerations
- **Call to Action**: Prominent signup or "Start Analyzing" button leading to account creation or demo access.
- **Visuals**: Use charts, graphs, and crypto-themed graphics to illustrate features like momentum scores and trend lines.
- **Mobile Responsiveness**: Ensure the landing page is optimized for mobile devices, as users may access it on-the-go.
- **SEO and Marketing**: Incorporate keywords like "crypto AI insights," "blockchain trends," "DeFi analysis" for better visibility.
- **Compliance**: Highlight adherence to data privacy standards, especially for financial insights.
